# Zad Land Project & UI Guidelines

## 1. Reference Design & Brand Identity
- The canonical visual reference designs for Zad Land are:
  - `theme.jpeg`: Official Desktop Layout & Showcase
  - `theme 2.jpeg`: Official Mobile Layout & B2B Cards
- All UI work on the store front must align with these mockups.

## 2. Design System & Palette Tokens
- **Primary Gold**: `#B8860B` (Hover/Dark Accent: `#E5B54A`, `#C5A059`)
- **Brand Navy / Deep Teal**: `#072835` (Used for key headings, brand accents)
- **Backgrounds**:
  - Light mode base: `#ffffff` with warm cream containers `#FAF6EC` / `#FAF9F5`
  - Dark mode base: `#1A1A14` with surface `#2A2A1E` / `#1E1E16`
- **Accent Green**: `#2E7D32` (Used for supply banners and wholesale highlights)
- **Typography**: Figtree (Latin) & Noto Sans Arabic (Arabic), with first-class RTL support.

## 3. B2B Wholesale Product Standards
- Zad Land is a **Wholesale Food & Goods Distributor (B2B)**, not a typical retail shop.
- Product listings and showcases must surface B2B-specific packaging details:
  1. **Pieces per carton** (`عدد القطع في الكرتون`)
  2. **Unit weight** (`الوزن`)
  3. **Country of Origin** (`بلد المنشأ` with country flag)
  4. **Wholesale Carton Price** (`سعر الجملة` in USD)
  5. **Direct quantity stepper** (`[-] qty [+]`) with instant Add-to-Cart button directly on cards.
- Pricing note: All prices are in USD and include VAT (`جميع الأسعار بالدولار الأمريكي وتشمل ضريبة القيمة المضافة`).

## 4. Layout & Ornamental Signatures
- **Section Headers**: Use the signature ornamental divider format:
  `─────── 🌾 [عنوان القسم] 🌾 ───────` with wheat stalk icons and gradient accent rules.
- **Wholesale Value Pillars**: The 4 core customer commitments:
  1. **جودة مضمونة**: منتجات أصلية 100%
  2. **توزيع في جميع المحافظات**: خدمة سريعة وموثوقة
  3. **أسعار تنافسية**: أفضل الأسعار للجملة
  4. **بيع بالجملة فقط**: للتجار وأصحاب المحلات
